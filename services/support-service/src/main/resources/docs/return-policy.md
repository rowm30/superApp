# Return Policy

Buyers can return most items within 14 days of delivery for a full refund.

Electronics (phones, laptops, headphones) must be returned within 7 days
and in original packaging with all accessories.

Items marked "Final Sale" cannot be returned.

To start a return, go to Order History, select the order, and click
"Request Return". Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after
we receive the item.

Shipping costs for returns are covered by the buyer unless the item
arrived damaged or defective.

# Shipping Policy

Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days. Express shipping (paid) takes
1-2 business days.

Orders placed before 2 PM are processed the same day.

# Order Cancellation

Orders can be cancelled free of charge within 1 hour of placing them,
as long as the seller hasn't shipped yet. After that, the buyer must
wait for delivery and initiate a return instead.
